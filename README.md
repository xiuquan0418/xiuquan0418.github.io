# Xiuquan Wang

Personal academic website of **Dr. Xiuquan Wang**, Associate Professor of Mathematics, Hazael McFarland (H. M.) Thompson Endowed Chair in Mathematics, and Chair of the Department of Mathematics & Computer Science at Tougaloo College.

**Website:** https://xiuquan0418.github.io/

## About

My research lies at the intersection of applied mathematics, computational biology, machine learning, and biomedical artificial intelligence.

Current research areas include:

- RNA velocity and cellular dynamics
- Single-cell transcriptomics and isoform-level analysis
- Nanopore long-read sequencing and DNA methylation
- Biological network analysis
- Machine learning for biomedical data

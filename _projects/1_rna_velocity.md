## Overview

RNA velocity provides information about the direction and dynamics of cellular state transitions using single-cell transcriptomic data.

Our research develops mathematical and computational approaches to evaluate RNA velocity representations, assess transition consistency, and improve the interpretation of cellular dynamics.

## Research Directions

- Evaluation of RNA velocity embeddings and transition consistency.
- Development of the Embedding Velocity Topology Score (EVTS).
- Analysis of branching trajectories and cellular state transitions.
- Applications to single-cell biomedical datasets.

## Research Significance

This project aims to improve the reliability and biological interpretation of RNA velocity analysis, supporting the study of dynamic processes in complex biological systems.

## Research

Our research integrates artificial intelligence, mathematical modeling, network science, and bioinformatics to investigate complex biological and biomedical systems. Current projects focus on cellular dynamics, high-dimensional biological data, large-scale networks, and biomedical artificial intelligence.

<div class="research-grid">

<div class="research-card">
<img src="{{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/images/research/evts.png" class="research-thumb" alt="RNA velocity embedding and cellular trajectories">
<div class="research-body">
<h4 class="research-title">EVTS: Topology-Aware Evaluation of RNA Velocity Embeddings</h4>
<p class="research-desc">We develop topology-aware methods to evaluate whether RNA velocity vectors projected into low-dimensional embeddings accurately represent future cellular states. EVTS and its extensions quantify transition consistency, neighborhood preservation, and trajectory stability, and provide a framework for refining RNA velocity visualizations.</p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="research-card">
<img src="{{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/images/research/brain-network.png" class="research-thumb" alt="Large-scale cell-cell similarity network">
<div class="research-body">
<h4 class="research-title">Mega-Scale Cell–Cell Similarity Networks</h4>
<p class="research-desc">We construct and analyze million-node cell–cell similarity networks from embryonic mouse brain transcriptomes. Large-scale graph analytics, community detection, and bridge-cell analysis are used to characterize transcriptional communities and identify potential transitional developmental states.</p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="research-card">
<img src="{{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/images/research/nsclc.png" class="research-thumb" alt="Macrophage state transitions in lung cancer">
<div class="research-body">
<h4 class="research-title">Macrophage State Transitions in NSCLC</h4>
<p class="research-desc">We integrate RNA velocity and isoform-level analysis to investigate macrophage state transitions in non-small cell lung cancer. This research seeks to characterize macrophage plasticity, transcriptional heterogeneity, and dynamic cellular states within the tumor microenvironment.</p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="research-card">
<img src="{{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/images/research/nanopore.png" class="research-thumb" alt="Nanopore long-read sequencing analysis">
<div class="research-body">
<h4 class="research-title">Machine Learning for Nanopore Long-Read Sequencing</h4>
<p class="research-desc">We develop machine learning and mathematical approaches to extract biological information from Nanopore long-read sequencing data. Research directions include isoform-level analysis, DNA methylation detection, RNA dynamics, and computational modeling of complex sequencing data.</p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="research-card">
<img src="{{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/images/research/eeg-transformer.png" class="research-thumb" alt="EEG and Transformer-based biomedical artificial intelligence">
<div class="research-body">
<h4 class="research-title">Transformer-Based EEG Analysis for Neurological Disease</h4>
<p class="research-desc">We investigate Transformer-based deep learning architectures for neurological disease classification using EEG recordings. Our research focuses on representation learning, cross-subject classification, and robust biomedical artificial intelligence, with applications to Parkinson's disease.</p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="research-card">
<img src="{{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/images/research/depression-mfnet.png" class="research-thumb" alt="Multimodal artificial intelligence for depression detection">
<div class="research-body">
<h4 class="research-title">Depression-MFNet: Multimodal Depression Detection</h4>
<p class="research-desc">We develop multimodal fusion architectures for cross-dataset depression detection using EEG, audio, video, and text. Depression-MFNet combines modality-specific encoders, multimodal fusion, and task-specific prediction heads to improve generalization across heterogeneous biomedical datasets.</p>
</div>
</div>
